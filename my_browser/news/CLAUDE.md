# CLAUDE.md: news

News is DEEper's news radar: it reads AI news from many independent sources, merges the posts about one event into a story, scores the stories and hands them to the bot and the app. Its second lane, `opps`, does the same for opportunities: hackathons, competitions, schools, internships, research programs, grants, each with its deadline. This file covers what it does, how to run and check it alone, what the other parts expect from it and what it expects from them. The repository's rules for working in one part are in the root CLAUDE.md; the stages are in `PLAN_News.md` and `PLAN_Opportunities.md` (their «Решения» record where DEEper differs from the plans). Paths below are relative to `my_browser/` unless they are repo-root files.

## What news does

Done: stage 0 (the part itself), stage 1 (`/news` in the bot), stage 2 (collection from feeds and Hugging Face Daily Papers), stage 3 (public Telegram channels), stage 4 (merging posts into stories), stage 5 (scoring and summaries by the LLM), stage 6 (delivery to the bot and «Радар»), stage 7 (settings and sources from Telegram), stage 8 (learning from 👍/👎), stage 9 (what was dropped, and bringing it back), stage 10 (a daily copy of the database). Opportunities: stage O0 (the lane in the database and the code, the profile), stage O1 (its sources), stage O2 (reading them with the LLM), stage O3 (delivery, reminders, the bot's commands), stage O4's code (the app's «Возможности», quiet sources in `/sources`, the lane in `/news`; its day on Railway is still to come).
- `news/db.py`: everything news keeps, in `REPORTS_DIR/news.sqlite` (WAL): `sources`, `items`, `stories`, `feedback`, `settings`, `state`, `spend`, and for opportunities `opportunities` (one row per story of the lane: type, deadline, conditions, fit, status), `opp_reminders` and `opps_spend`. Columns newer than a table go into `ADDED_COLUMNS`, so an older file on the volume gets them.
- **Lanes.** A source, its posts and the stories made of them carry `lane`: `news` (everything before the opportunities) or `opps`. Posts merge only within their lane; the news part's scoring, delivery, feed, `/dropped`, feedback and the news fields of `/status` read only `lane = 'news'`. A new query on `stories` or `items` must say its lane.
- `news/sources.py`: the sources table and the starting list (`DEFAULTS`).
  - The list mixes kinds of sources: labs' own blogs (first-hand, `is_primary`), papers, independent media, analysts, communities, Russian sites and 26 public Telegram channels (`owner` `tg:<channel>`). Each was checked on 24.10.2026 (`PLAN_News.md`, «Решения»).
  - Each source has an `owner`, the organization behind it. Four Anthropic feeds or two Habr hubs are one owner, so a story counts independent owners, not feeds.
  - `ai_filter` marks a general feed (Techmeme, NVIDIA, 3DNews); only its posts about AI will be kept.
  - `interval` is minutes between polls, by category (`INTERVALS`); Reddit gets 60, since it answers 429 to frequent requests.
  - Defaults are offered once (`state.offered_defaults`): a source the user removes does not come back, and one added to `DEFAULTS` later reaches existing databases at the next start.
  - Sites without RSS come through feeds generated by github.com/Olshansk/rss-feeds (`GENERATED`). Meta AI's was 90 days old on 24.10.2026: its generator may be broken.
  - The opportunities lane's part (`_opp()`, `_opp_tg()`): 58 sources of 49 owners checked on 25.10.2026 (`PLAN_Opportunities.md`, «Решения», has the survey and what was refused): aggregators of hackathons and competitions, Devpost's online hackathons, awesome-mlss's schools, AI safety programs, companies' and ITMO's channels, conferences and grants. Categories `aggregator`, `company`, `university`, `program`, `conference`, `community`, polled by `OPPS_INTERVALS`; a page once a day (`PAGE_INTERVAL`). Several channels of one company share its owner (`yandex`, `sber`, `itmo`). The faculty's and the lab's channels are deliberately not here: the user adds them with `/add opps`.
  - `listing()` (`GET /sources`) gives each source its health and `week` (fresh posts of the last week), and an enabled source of the opps lane `silent_days` when no new post has been seen for longer than its kind usually stays quiet (`SILENT_DAYS`: an aggregator 14 days, a page 90, any other category `SILENT_DAYS_OTHER` 45); else `silent_days` is null. Silence counts from the newest post's `seen` (backfill included), so a new source's count starts at its first poll.
- `news/config.py`: the user's settings, defaults in `Settings`, changes in the `settings` table. Every value is checked; a refused change raises `SettingsError` with a message for the user in Russian (`explain()`), and a stored value that no longer passes is deleted at load, so its default applies.
  - The `opps_` settings are the opportunities lane's: `opps_enabled`, the weekly digest (`opps_digest_day`, `opps_digest_time`), «hot» ones sent at once (`opps_hot_days`, `opps_hot_min_fit`), `opps_reminders` (days before a tracked deadline), `opps_types` (`OPP_TYPES`), `opps_budget_month_rub` (30 ₽, apart from the news budget; a tenth of it a day) and `opps_profile`. `paused` stops only news: deadlines keep coming.
  - `opps_profile` is the text each opportunity's fit is scored against. The default is deliberately neutral: the repository is public, and the user's own profile goes in through the bot's `/profile`. `clean_profile()` drops emails, links, handles and phone numbers before it reaches a prompt.
- `news/collectors/`: one module per kind of source, `async fetch(source, client) -> Fetched`.
  - `rss.py`: RSS and Atom (feedparser, parsed in a thread), with conditional GET (`etag`, `modified`): an unchanged feed answers 304.
  - `page.py`: a page without a feed (a program, a company's internships, an aggregator's list), once a day. Its main text (no menus, headers, footers, forms) is fingerprinted into `sources.page_hash`, the text kept in `page_text`. The first poll stores the page as a post; a program's page that changed gives its whole new text with «Изменилось на странице: …» at the end (the embedding reads the unchanged start, so the post joins its story), an aggregator's list (category `aggregator`) only its new lines, as a digest. A change only in numbers, in lines that say nothing of deadlines or applications (`DEADLINE_WORDS`), is remembered without a post. Less than `MIN_TEXT` (200 characters) of text: a JavaScript site, refused.
  - `devpost.py`: Devpost's online hackathons through the JSON its pages read (`/api/hackathons`, nine a page, `MAX_PAGES`); a post per hackathon with its organizer, submission period, prizes and themes, dated when first seen.
  - `mlss.py`: the data file of awesome-mlss (machine learning summer schools): a post per school whose deadline has not passed, its id carrying the deadline, so a moved deadline is a new post. PyYAML comes with gpt-researcher.
  - `hf_papers.py`: the official Daily Papers API, papers with `MIN_UPVOTES` (20) or more, linked to arXiv. The whole arXiv cs.CL feed was left out: 183 papers a day.
  - `telegram.py`: public channels through the web preview `t.me/s/<channel>` (the last ~20 posts, no account). The first line stands in for a title; links come from the text, the link preview and a forward's original, so one news item reposted by several channels shares a key. The selectors are the page's HTML of 24.10.2026: if Telegram changes it, every channel fails at once with «no public preview» or yields no posts.
  - `__init__.py`: `Post`, `text_and_links()`, `link_key()` and `usable_key()`. A post keeps the keys of the pages it links to, without the scheme, `www.`, tracking and referral parameters, share buttons and its own site's pages; arXiv's abs, pdf and html pages are one key. `usable_key()` says which keys may merge posts: a specific page, not a front page (a shared bare `z.ai` once joined two unrelated posts), a section like `/news`, a profile, a repository's owner or a newsletter's footer (`NEWSLETTER`: MarkTechPost's beehiiv form glued 4–6 of its posts).
  - A Telegram post with 4 or more links is a digest (`Post.digest`): a channel's roundup of several stories, stored but kept out of merging. Linking every story it retells, it would glue them together.
- `news/items.py`: storing posts. `(source_id, external_id)` is unique, so a poll never stores a post twice.
  - Backfill: a source's first poll brings its whole history (OpenAI lists 1 300 posts). Posts older than the dedup window, and undated posts of the first poll, are stored as `backfill`: known, so they never come back as new, and never news.
  - `ai_filter` sources keep only posts that name AI (`is_about_ai()`: acronyms in capitals, words and model names in both languages).
  - `slim()`, daily: posts older than their lane's `KEEP_TEXT_DAYS` (news 30, opps 180) lose their text and embedding but keep their rows, so a feed that still lists them does not bring them back.
  - A post takes its source's lane. In the opps lane posts stay fresh for `OPPS_FRESH_DAYS` (30), on the first poll too: an announcement of a month ago may still be open.
- `news/embed.py`: sentence embeddings (fastembed, `MODEL` = paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2, 384 dimensions) in a child process (`python -m my_browser.news.embed`), so the model's few hundred megabytes go back after each batch. It is downloaded once (220 MB) into `REPORTS_DIR/.models`, or `NEWS_MODELS_DIR`. About 8 s for 300 posts on the Mac once downloaded.
- `news/dedup.py`: merging posts into stories, every 5 minutes (`loop.DEDUP_EVERY_SEC`), oldest post first.
  - A usable link shared with a post of an open story (one with a post in the dedup window) merges, if the two are not unrelated (`LINK_FLOOR` 0.35).
  - Otherwise by meaning: the story whose first post is as close as `dedup_threshold` (0.66, chosen by `eval/news`), or whose closest post is `CHAIN_MARGIN` (0.08) closer still. Measuring from the first post keeps a story from drifting through a chain of posts each like the one before.
  - A story that has a post of the same owner takes another one only by a shared link or when it is very close: an outlet's different articles on one theme are alike.
  - A post that shares links with several stories joins the one with the most shared links, then the closest (`_nearest()`). Before 25.10.2026 that tie hung on the hash seed of the process: the same posts merged differently from run to run.
  - Each lane merges apart (`_run()`). The opps lane takes posts of the last `items.OPPS_FRESH_DAYS`, and its stories stay open `OPPS_OPEN_DAYS` (60) after their last post or while their opportunity is open (`open`, `tracked`, `applied`), never once it `expired`.
  - The opps lane merges more strictly, since announcements follow templates: by meaning only posts of different owners, from `OPPS_THRESHOLD` (0.85; aggregators' reposts of one announcement were 0.86–1.00 apart, unrelated posts up to 0.79, and under the news rules 69 Devpost hackathons became one story); posts of one owner only by a shared link; a watched page's new version joins its previous story; a link in `BOILERPLATE_POSTS` (4) or more posts of one source and nobody else's is its footer and merges nothing (`_boilerplate()`). A retelling in other words is the model's to merge (stage O2). On the collection of 25.10.2026: 333 stories, 8 merges, all right.
  - `source_count` counts distinct owners; `has_primary` a first-hand source; `title` is the first-hand post's, else the earliest of the best kind (`RANK`), until stage 5 writes `title_ru`.
  - When the model cannot run (not downloaded, out of memory), posts are merged by shared links only and stored without an embedding: stories and digests go on (284 posts made 248 stories that way on the same collection).
  - On the collection of 24.10.2026: 287 posts made 218 stories, among them Kimi K3.1 with 14 independent sources, OpenAI's offline training with 7 and the first-hand post.
- `news/scoring.py`: the LLM, after each merging run, within the budget.
  - Flash scores the stories that wait (never scored, or grown by `RESCORE_GROWTH` sources), `SCORE_BATCH` (12) a call: `is_ai`, `topic` (`config.TOPICS`), `importance` 0–10 with a reason, `title_ru`, `primary_url` (a first-hand link among the posts', which also sets `has_primary`).
  - Not about AI, or below `MIN_IMPORTANCE` (4): `dropped` with `drop_reason`; else `queued`. A sent story stays `sent`.
  - `same_as`: each story comes with the `SIMILAR_REFS` (3) scored stories closest to it by post embeddings (`_similar()`, from `SIMILAR_FLOOR` 0.45); when the model names one, or an earlier story of the batch, the story is merged into it (`merge_into()`: posts moved, status `merged`). It catches what embeddings alone could not: two stories about one OpenAI round were 0.72 apart, two different ones 0.67.
  - Summaries in Russian, one or two sentences: Pro from `PRO_FROM` (7), Flash below, `SUMMARY_BATCH` (6) a call.
  - `is_opportunity`: the same scoring call says whether a story announces something to apply to; its posts are then copied into the opps lane (`copy_to_opps()`: one per owner, up to `POSTS_PER_STORY`, `copied_from` pointing to the original), where merging and `opps.py` read them. The news story itself is scored as usual.
  - Every call waits at most `ASK_TIMEOUT_SEC` (300 s), then is asked once more: on 25.10.2026 a call through the Mac's VPN hung for 20 minutes, and in the loop that would hold every delivery.
  - Every call is metered (`common/costs.py`) into `spend` by day in the settings' time zone (the opportunities lane's into `opps_spend`: `SPEND`, `spent(settings, lane)`, `within_budget(settings, lane)`); `within_budget()` stops calls at the day's limit (`daily_budget_rub`: a fifteenth of `budget_month_rub` (100 ₽), twice an average day, so the backlog after a deploy and a day's news both fit) or the month's. `paused` stops them too.
  - Measured on the collection of 24.10.2026 (218 stories, off-peak): the whole backlog cost 2.8 ₽ in 41 calls, 113 stories queued, 105 dropped (opinions, ads, tutorials, not AI), 4 merged by `same_as`. A day's new stories should cost about 1–1.5 ₽.
- `news/opps.py`: the opportunities lane's LLM (Flash), after news scoring in each merging run, within the lane's budget (`opps_spend`, `scoring.within_budget(settings, "opps")`); `opps_enabled` off stops it.
  - `split()`: a roundup (a digest post of the lane: a channel's «5 хакатонов недели», an aggregator page's list, AISafety.com's weekly digest) is cut into one post per opportunity, each with its own address where the roundup gives one (`items.copied_from` points to the roundup, `split_at` marks it done); the next merging run places the parts like any post. A part without an address of its own keeps the roundup's, which merges nothing (`dedup.OWN_URL`).
  - `extract()`: each waiting story (never read, or grown since: a new post may carry a moved deadline), `EXTRACT_BATCH` (6) a call, up to `POSTS_PER_STORY` (3) posts each, the newest first: is it an opportunity, its type (`config.OPP_TYPES`), Russian title, organizer, summary, the deadline as written and as ISO with its time zone, `deadline_kind`, `closed` (the text says applications are closed), the event's dates, format, place, eligibility, `ru_access`, cost, reward, where to apply, `fit` 0–10 against the profile (`config.clean_profile()`) with a reason, and `same_as` among up to `SIMILAR_REFS` read opportunities closest by embeddings (merged like news, `scoring.merge_into()`).
  - A story with no sign of an announcement in its posts (`SIGNAL`) is dropped without a call: a quarter of the collection of 25.10.2026, none of eval's opportunities. Pages, Devpost and awesome-mlss always go to the model (`ALWAYS_READ`).
  - `deadline()` checks dates, never trusts them: ISO only; the zone named in the text (AoE, UTC±N, PT, MSK, an IANA name), else Moscow's for a Russian source and UTC for an international one (`tz_assumed`); a date alone is the end of that day; a year off by more than a few is dropped. `apply()` stores the row: `dropped` (not an opportunity, with the reason), `expired` (deadline past, or `closed`), else `open`; `tracked`, `hidden` and `applied`, the user's, are kept. A tracked opportunity whose deadline moved by more than a day queues «🗓 Дедлайн перенесён» (`deliveries`, kind `opp_deadline`). `expire()` runs every time: passed deadlines become `expired`.
  - `read()` is the one extraction call, which `eval/opps/run_extract.py` measures with the same prompt and parsing; `listing()` serves `GET /opps`.
- `news/opps_delivery.py`: opportunities to Telegram, every tick after the news (`run()`), into the same `deliveries`.
  - Hot: a new open opportunity due within `opps_hot_days` with a fit from `opps_hot_min_fit` goes at once (several in one message, `HOT_MAX`); in the quiet hours it waits.
  - The weekly digest on `opps_digest_day` at `opps_digest_time` (within `DIGEST_LATE_HOURS`; done dates in `state.opps_digests_done`): the new open ones not sent yet, `DIGEST_MAX` shown and the rest counted, ordered by `order()`: how soon (two weeks, a month, later, unknown), then fit; low fit and no access from Russia last. Once a month (`state.opps_check_month`) it asks about tracked ones without a date.
  - «How soon» is `effective()`: the deadline, else the event's start, since hackathons often give only their dates. An opportunity without a deadline whose event has begun expires (`opps.expire()`).
  - Reminders of tracked deadlines `opps_reminders` days ahead by calendar days in the settings' time zone (so «за 1 день» comes the day before, after the quiet hours), each once (`opp_reminders`), one message when several are due (tracked late); approximate deadlines say so; rolling and unknown get none; applied, hidden and expired nothing.
  - Cards (`card()`): «🏁 Хакатон · дедлайн 12 ноя (через 18 дней) · онлайн · ✅ из РФ», the title and organizer, what it gives, «Почему тебе: …», «🔗 подача»; lists use `line()`. Only `opps_types` go out.
  - `set_status()` is the user's choice (`tracked`, `hidden`, `applied`, `open`); `tracked()` feeds `/deadlines`; `by_date()` orders `/opps`.
  - `dressed()` adds what the bot and the app show, so neither formats anything itself: `line` and `card` (Telegram HTML), `meta` (the app's plain caption, `meta()`: «Хакатон · дедлайн 12 ноя (через 18 дней) · онлайн · из РФ: да», words instead of the bot's colored marks, which the brandbook's palette lacks), `type_name`, `type_short` (`SHORT`, short enough for the app's filter on a phone) and `apply` (where to apply, else the latest post's link). `when()` is the «how soon» part, which `/status` also gives.
- `news/delivery.py`: what goes to Telegram and when; the bot sends it, «Радар» reads stories through `/feed`.
  - Every tick `run()` queues what is due into `deliveries` (Telegram HTML and buttons), and the bot takes them (`/deliveries`) and marks them (`/deliveries/{id}/sent`).
  - A digest at each `digest_times` slot (within `DIGEST_LATE_HOURS`, so a restart does not lose it; done slots in `state.digests_done`): the queued stories, the most important first, at most `digest_max_stories`, with «🔎 N» buttons (`nr:<story>`). It is built before the urgent ones, so a due digest takes them.
  - Urgent: a queued story from `urgent_min_importance` (8), first seen within `URGENT_HOURS` and summarized, at once with «🔎 Исследовать / 👍 / 👎» (`nf:<story>:±1`); several at once in one message. In the quiet hours it waits; right after them, when a digest is due within `DIGEST_HOLD_HOURS` (08:00 → 09:00), it waits for that digest and leads it.
  - A sent story that grew to twice its `sent_count` and `UPDATE_MIN` more comes back once in a digest as «🔄 обновление» (`update_sent`).
  - A story: «🟢 первоисточник · 7 источников · модели» (🟡 пересказы without a first-hand source), the Russian title, the summary, «🔗 первоисточник · ещё 6». The app's `header` has no colored marks: green and yellow are not in its brand's palette.
  - Checked with a simulated clock on real scored stories: nothing at 03:00 and 08:30, a digest of 15 at 09:00 (6 000 characters: the bot splits it in two), nothing again at 09:30, an urgent story alone at 12:00, the evening digest of the rest at 18:00.
- `news/manage.py`: sources the user adds and removes, to the news lane or, with `lane="opps"`, to the opportunities (as `aggregator`; an address without a feed but with text becomes a watched `page` of category `program`). A channel is matched case-insensitively (`@Gonzo_ML` is the starting `gonzo_ML`). `add()` checks a feed (it must parse and have posts) or a channel (a public preview with posts) before storing it as `media`, its owner the feed's site or `tg:<channel>`; an existing one is switched back on. `switch()` only switches a source off: its posts stay in their stories, and a starting source switched off is not offered again. Refusals raise `SourceError` with a message for the user.
- Only the topics on in the settings reach digests, urgent messages and «Радар» (`delivery._topics()`); scoring still scores everything.
- `news/feedback.py`: 👍/👎 of the last `LEARN_DAYS` (30) days summed per topic and per source owner, `STEP` (0.25) importance points a vote, at most `MAX_SHIFT` (2). `rank()` gives each story its `effective` importance (scored + topic's + the mean of its owners'), by which digests, urgent messages and «Радар» order and filter. Computed each time, never written into settings or sources. Four 👎 on «бизнес» moved its stories from places 3, 5, 6 to 14, 13, 16 of the feed (at 0.5 a vote they fell out altogether).
- `delivery.dropped()` and `restore()`: what scoring dropped, with reasons; a story brought back («Это было важно») goes out at once in a message of its own and counts as 👍.
- `db.backup()`, daily with `items.slim()`: a copy of `news.sqlite` in `REPORTS_DIR/backups`, the last 7 kept. Same volume: it guards against a broken file or a bad change, not against losing the volume.
- `news/loop.py`: the news loop, a plain thread that ticks every minute and marks each tick (`state.loop_tick`).
  - Each tick polls the due sources, at most `CONCURRENCY` (6) at once and one request per host at a time, `HOST_GAP_SEC` (2 s) apart: Reddit answers 429 to quicker ones.
  - A failing source waits twice its interval after one failure, four times after two, eight times after three or more (`sources.due()`).
  - `collect()` is shared with `POST /collect` and never runs twice at once (`_collecting`).
- `news/api.py`: the FastAPI app behind the service token: `/status`, `/sources`, `/items`, `/sources` (GET, POST), `/sources/find`, `/sources/{id}` (DELETE), `/stories` (`lane=news|opps`), `/stories/{id}`, `/feed`, `/opps` (`status`, `type`, `limit`), `/opps/run`, `/opps/tracked`, `/opps/{id}`, `/opps/{id}/status`, `/deliveries`, `/deliveries/{id}/sent`, `/feedback`, `/dropped`, `/stories/{id}/restore`, `/collect`, `/dedup`, `/score`, `/deliver`, `/settings` (GET, PUT), `/health`.

## Run and check it alone

```bash
uv run python -m my_browser.news      # API at 127.0.0.1:8504 (NEWS_PORT) + the news loop
```

- With a throwaway `REPORTS_DIR` and your own `NEWS_PORT`, call it with the service token of that directory: `curl -s localhost:<port>/status -H "Authorization: Bearer $(cat <REPORTS_DIR>/.service_token)"`.
- A wrong setting: `curl -X PUT .../settings -d '{"digest_times": ["25:00"]}'` answers 422 with the message the bot will show.
- **Collection.** The loop polls every source at its first tick (60 sources in about 30 s on 24.10.2026: 224 fresh posts of the last 48 hours, 5 147 rows with backfill). `curl -X POST '.../collect?everything=true'` polls them all again: it should store only what is really new. `/items` lists the latest fresh posts, `/sources` each source's `last_error` and `fails`.
- **Scoring.** `curl -X POST .../score` scores and summarizes what waits, with the real LLM unless the mock's environment is set; `dev/mock_llm.py` answers both prompts (the first story gets 9, so a free run reaches urgent delivery). To see real quality, run `scoring.run()` on a copy of a real `news.sqlite` with a raised `budget_month_rub` on the copy: the whole backlog of a day costs about 3 ₽.
- **Opportunities.** `curl -X POST .../opps/run` cuts the waiting roundups and reads the waiting stories now (real Flash unless the mock's environment is set; the mock answers both prompts), `/opps` lists what is open, `/stories?lane=opps` the stories with their posts. `uv run eval/opps/run_extract.py` measures the reading on labeled real posts (about 1 ₽; see `eval/CLAUDE.md`). On a copy of a real `news.sqlite` two runs read about 120 stories for 2 ₽.
- **Merging.** `curl -X POST .../dedup` merges the waiting posts now, `/stories` lists the stories with their posts. `uv run eval/news/run_dedup.py` measures merging on `eval/news/pairs.jsonl`, for free (it downloads the models into `eval/reports/.models` the first time); see `eval/CLAUDE.md`.
- From a Mac the requests go out through the VPN, and Railway's address is a data center's: a source may answer differently there (Reddit limits data centers). `/news` in the bot names the failing sources.
- The all-in-one app starts the loop too; `NEWS_LOOP=0` keeps it off there (the API still answers).
- **The whole project.** Before committing, run the `check-project` skill.

## What others rely on

- `app.py` and `news/__main__.py` start `loop.run_forever()` in a thread.
- `common/clients.call("news", ...)` reaches the API in-process unless `NEWS_URL` is set.
- `/status` → `sources` (`total`, `enabled`, `ok`, `pending`, `failing` with `name`, `error`, `fails`), `items` and `stories` (`day`, `total`), `spend` (`today_rub`, `month_rub`, `budget_month_rub`, `daily_budget_rub`), `last_collect` (or null; `at` in the settings' time zone, `new`), `loop_alive`, `telegram`, `settings`; all of the news lane. The bot's `/news` (`bot/telegram.news_text()`) reads all of them but `total`, `telegram` and `daily_budget_rub`.
- `/status` → `opps`: `enabled`, `sources` (the same fields, of the lane), `open` (open or tracked), `tracked`, `next_deadline` and `next_tracked` (or null: `story_id`, `title_ru`, `deadline`, `deadline_at`, `deadline_kind`, `starts`, `status`, and `when` from `opps_delivery.when()`), `spend` (the lane's, same fields). The bot's `/news` (`opps_text()`) reads `enabled`, `open`, `tracked`, `next_tracked` (`title_ru`, `when`), `sources` (`enabled`, `ok`, `failing`) and `spend` (`month_rub`, `budget_month_rub`).
- The bot's opportunity commands: `/opps` reads `GET /opps?limit=` (`story_id`, `status`, `opp_type`, `line`), `/deadlines` `GET /opps/tracked` (`story_id`, `line`), `/opptypes` reads and puts `opps_types` (with `opp_type_names`). The buttons the news part writes and the bot handles: `op:<story>:t|h|a|o|r` (track, not mine, applied, stop tracking, research: `POST /opps/{id}/status {status}` → the opportunity with `title_ru`, `title`, `deadline_at`, `deadline_kind`; 404 when there is none, 422 for another status; «r» reads `GET /opps/{id}`). Deliveries of kinds `opp_hot`, `opp_digest`, `opp_reminder`, `opp_deadline` go through `deliver_news()` like the news. All within 64 bytes.
- The bot's `/profile` reads `opps_profile` from `GET /settings` and replaces it with `PUT /settings {"opps_profile": text}` (422 below 20 or above 3 000 characters). `GET /settings` also gives `topic_names`, `opp_type_names` and `weekday_names`.
- `PUT /settings {key: value}` → the settings, or 422 with `detail` in Russian, one line per refused value.
- The bot (`bot/telegram.py`):
  - `deliver_news()` polls `GET /deliveries` → `[{id, kind, created, text, buttons}]` every 30 s, sends `text` (Telegram HTML; each line closes its own tags, so `_chunks()` may split between lines) with `buttons` (rows of `[label, callback data]`) on the last part, then `POST /deliveries/{id}/sent`.
  - `/dropped` reads `GET /dropped?hours=&limit=` (`id`, `title_ru`, `title`, `drop_reason`, `importance`), and its «↩️ N» (`nd:<story id>`) posts `/stories/{id}/restore` (404 when it is not dropped any more).
  - Callback data the news part writes: `nr:<story id>` (the bot reads `GET /stories/{id}`: `title_ru`, `title` and offers it as a research topic) and `nf:<story id>:<1|-1>` (`POST /feedback {story_id, value}`). Both stay within Telegram's 64 bytes.
- The bot's settings commands: `GET /settings` (with `topic_names`) and `PUT /settings` (422 `detail` shown as it is); `/sources` reads `GET /sources` (`id`, `name`, `kind`, `url`, `lane`, `enabled`, `polled`, `last_ok`, `last_error`, `week`, `silent_days`; never `page_text`), groups it by lane and marks a quiet source without an error 💤; `/add` posts `{text, lane}` to `POST /sources` (422 `detail` on refusal, also for a lane other than `news` and `opps`); `/remove` finds with `GET /sources/find?q=` and switches off with `DELETE /sources/{id}`.
- Core's «Радар» (`core/ui/radar.py`): `GET /feed?limit=` → stories with `id`, `title_ru`, `title`, `summary_ru`, `header` (no colored marks), `link`, `link_label`, `source_count`, `importance`; its «Возможности» tab `GET /opps?limit=` (open and tracked, the tracked first) → `story_id`, `status`, `opp_type`, `type_short`, `type_name`, `title_ru`, `title`, `meta`, `fit_reason`, `summary_ru`, `apply`. `tabs.page()` fetches both before building the page.

## What news relies on

- **`common`**: `settings` (`REPORTS_DIR`, `NEWS_URL`), `auth`, `clients`. News imports no module of core, the worker, search or the bot.
- **The bot's token variables**: `/status` reports `telegram` from `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` / `TELEGRAM_TOKEN`, the same variables `bot/telegram.token()` reads.
- **DeepSeek**, through gpt-researcher's `create_chat_completion` with `Config(CONFIG_PATH)` (`FAST_LLM`, `SMART_LLM`, `LLM_KWARGS`), like search's rating; `common/costs.py` patches it when imported, which is what meters the calls. The writer toggle of researches does not reach news.
- **The sources themselves**: their feeds' addresses and formats. Sites without RSS come through github.com/Olshansk/rss-feeds, whose generators can stop (Meta AI's was 90 days old). Hugging Face's `/api/daily_papers` fields: `paper.id`, `title`, `summary`, `upvotes`, `submittedOnDailyAt`, `githubRepo`, `projectPage`.

## State news owns

- `REPORTS_DIR/news.sqlite`, next to core's reports; core's `sync()` indexes only `<date>_*.json`, so it is left alone.
- One loop per `REPORTS_DIR`: two loops would poll every source twice. The table's unique keys keep the data right, but the sources see double the requests.
- `items` grows by every post ever seen, backfill included (5 000 rows after the first poll); `slim()` keeps old rows small.

| Change | Also update |
|---|---|
| a field of `/status` | the bot's `news_text()`, and for `opps` its `opps_text()` |
| a query on `stories` or `items` | its lane: news code reads `lane = 'news'`, so a story of the opps lane never reaches a digest |
| a setting | `LABELS` in `config.py`; the bot's commands and the app once they change it (stage 7) |
| the starting sources | `PLAN_News.md`, «Решения», if the mix of kinds changes |
| a table or column | `ADDED_COLUMNS` for databases already on the volume |
| `link_key()` or `usable_key()` | merging changes: rerun `eval/news/run_dedup.py`; keys already stored keep the old form |
| the embedding model or `embed.text()` | rerun the eval and choose `dedup_threshold` again: similarities of another model are not comparable; stored embeddings of open stories are of the old model until the window passes |
| a merging rule in `dedup.py` | run it on a copy of a real `news.sqlite` and read the biggest stories (`dedup.stories()`) |
| a scoring or summary prompt | `dev/mock_llm.py` recognizes them by «Ты редактор ленты новостей об искусственном интеллекте» and «Напиши по-русски резюме каждого сюжета»; check quality on a copy of real data |
| `opps.EXTRACT_PROMPT`, `describe()` or `deadline()` | rerun `eval/opps/run_extract.py` (about 1 ₽, ask first) and read its misses; `dev/mock_llm.py` recognizes the prompts by «Ты разбираешь анонсы возможностей для студента» and «Ты разбираешь посты-подборки о возможностях» |

| an opportunity status | `dedup._windows()` (which stories stay open), `opps.listing()`, `/status` (`open`, `tracked`), `opps_delivery` |

| an opportunity button's callback data | the bot's `on_opportunity()`, `on_opps_filter()`, `on_opptype_toggle()` |
| a story status | `delivery.py` and `/status` read `queued`, `sent`, `dropped`, `merged` |
| a delivery's text, buttons or callback data | the bot's `deliver_news()`, `on_news_research()`, `on_news_feedback()` |
| a field of `/feed` | `core/ui/radar.py` |
